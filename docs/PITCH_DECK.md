# Pitch Deck: Puno at Halaman AI 🌿

---

## Slide 1: Title
**Puno at Halaman AI**
*AI-Assisted Philippine Plant, Tree & Herbal Knowledge Identifier*
"Bridging AI Computer Vision with Philippine Biodiversity Conservation"

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
- The Philippines is one of 17 megadiverse countries, harboring over 10,000 plant species.
- **Challenge:** Rapid loss of local botanical knowledge, field identification bottlenecks for foresters, and accidental poisoning from toxic look-alike plants.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
- An easy-to-use mobile-first web app tailored specifically to the Philippines.
- Upload photos of leaves, bark, flowers, or whole trees to get:
  - Scientific & local Tagalog names
  - Native / Endemic / Invasive classification
  - Evidence-graded herbal & safety warnings
  - Geo-referenced field observation recording

---

## Slide 4: Key Differentiators
1. **RAG Database Knowledge:** Doesn't rely solely on AI vision; cross-checks candidates against local botanical sources.
2. **Scientific Safety First:** Prominently warns against poisonous plants and distinguishes folklore from clinical evidence.
3. **Bilingual UI:** Native support for English and Tagalog/Filipino.

---

## Slide 5: Business & Impact Model
- **Institutional Subscriptions:** SaaS licensing for forestry firms, ecotourism operators, and academic institutions.
- **Government Partnerships:** DENR forest monitoring contracts and TESDA educational module licensing.
