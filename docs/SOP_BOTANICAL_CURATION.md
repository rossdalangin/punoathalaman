# Standard Operating Procedure: Botanical Database Curation & Knowledge Management

**SOP Code:** SOP-PH-003
**Version:** 1.0
**Target Audience:** Database Curators, Ethnobotany Researchers, Botanical Content Managers

---

## 1. Objective
To maintain the scientific integrity, pharmacological evidence grading, and conservation accuracy of the internal database.

## 2. Evidence Grading Guidelines

### 2.1 Native / Endemic Status Classification
- Must cite authoritative sources: DENR Biodiversity Management Bureau (BMB), Plants of the World Online (POWO), or GBIF.
- **ENDEMIC**: Species natively occurring exclusively in the Philippines.
- **NATIVE**: Species occurring naturally in the Philippines and neighboring region without human introduction.
- **INTRODUCED / NATURALIZED**: Alien species brought by human intervention.

### 2.2 Herbal & Medicinal Evidence Classification
- **ESTABLISHED**: Officially recognized by DOH PITAHC or published in peer-reviewed clinical trials.
- **SUPPORTED BY SOME RESEARCH**: In-vitro or preliminary animal models exist.
- **TRADITIONAL USE ONLY**: Ethnobotanical documentation without clinical validation.

### 2.3 Safety & Toxicity Classifications
- Every entry must specify safety classification (`SAFE_FOR_GENERAL_CONTACT`, `CAUTION`, `POTENTIALLY_TOXIC`, `KNOWN_POISONOUS_PLANT`).
- Poisonous plants (e.g., *Jatropha curcas*) must include mandatory warnings in Tagalog and English.
