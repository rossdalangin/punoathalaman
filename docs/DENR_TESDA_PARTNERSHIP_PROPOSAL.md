# Institutional Partnership Proposal: DENR, TESDA & LGU Collaboration

**Project Name:** Puno at Halaman AI
**Subject:** Digital Infrastructure for National Biodiversity Monitoring & Botanical Education

---

## Executive Summary
We respectfully propose a strategic partnership between **Puno at Halaman AI** and national government agencies (**DENR-BMB**, **TESDA**, **DOH-PITAHC**) and Local Government Units (LGUs) to deploy AI-assisted plant identification and community science field logging across the Philippines.

---

## Strategic Alignment

### 1. DENR Biodiversity Management Bureau (BMB)
- **Objective:** Enhanced monitoring of native and endemic flora, early detection of invasive alien species, and protected area canopy surveys.
- **Integration:** Deployment of field observation GPS logging tool (`/observations`) for forest rangers and community wildlife volunteer groups.

### 2. Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA)
- **Objective:** Modernization of Agriculture, Forestry, and Environmental Management TVET courses.
- **Integration:** Adoption of `/educational` competency modules and interactive visual drills for student skills assessment.

### 3. DOH PITAHC & Community Health Units
- **Objective:** Promoting botanical safety awareness and public education regarding traditional medicinal plants.
- **Integration:** Distribution of evidence-graded herbal profiles with mandatory toxicity warnings for dangerous look-alike species.

---

## Roadmap & Implementation Phases
- **Phase 1 (Months 1-3):** Pilot deployment in selected National Parks and TESDA regional training centers.
- **Phase 2 (Months 4-6):** Local database expansion with regional botanical herbarium records.
- **Phase 3 (Months 7-12):** Nationwide rollout and integration into Barangay Biodiversity Inventories.
