# User Tutorial Guide: How to Identify Philippine Plants with AI

Welcome to **Puno at Halaman AI**! This tutorial will guide you step-by-step through identifying plants, understanding results, and documenting species.

---

## 📸 Step 1: Taking or Selecting Quality Photos
For best AI accuracy:
- **Leaf Close-Up**: Photograph 1-2 mature leaves against a plain background.
- **Lighting**: Use bright natural daylight. Avoid harsh flash glare or dark shadows.
- **Focus**: Ensure leaf edges (margins) and vein patterns (venation) are crisp and clear.

---

## 📤 Step 2: Uploading Your Image
1. Open the homepage (`/`).
2. Choose your method:
   - Click **📷 Take Photo** on your mobile phone.
   - Click **🖼 Upload Image** to pick a saved screenshot or photo.
   - Click **📁 Upload Multiple Photos** if you have photos of leaf, bark, and flowers.
3. (Optional) Select environment details under **Optional Context** (e.g., location, flowers present, milky sap).
4. Click **Suriin at Kilalanin ang Halaman (Identify Plant)**.

---

## 🔍 Step 3: Reading Your Result Card
Your result card contains:
- **Most Likely Identification**: Primary common name and scientific name.
- **Confidence Level**: High (80%+), Moderate (50-79%), or Low (<50%).
- **Scientific Classification**: Kingdom, Family, Genus, and Species breakdown.
- **Philippine Context**: Native, Endemic, or Introduced status + Philippine regional distribution.
- **Herbal & Safety Warnings**: Clear distinction between traditional use and scientific evidence, plus prominent warnings for poisonous plants.
- **How to Verify Checklist**: Steps to confirm the plant identity yourself.

---

## 💾 Step 4: Saving a Field Record
If you are doing field research or community logging:
1. Click **💾 Record Field Observation** at the bottom of the result card.
2. Fill in your location (Province, Municipality, Barangay, GPS coordinates).
3. Save the entry for inclusion in community biodiversity reports!
