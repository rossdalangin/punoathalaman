# TESDA Educational Guide: Plant Identification Training Modules

**Target Alignment:** TESDA Agriculture, Forestry & Environmental Conservation Training Programs
**Module Code:** TESDA-PH-BOT-2026

---

## 📚 Competency Framework Overview
This guide provides instructional outlines for integrating "Puno at Halaman AI" into vocational and educational training.

---

## Module 1: Leaf Morphology & Botanical Nomenclature
- **Learning Objective:** Students identify leaf shapes, margin types, apex types, and venation patterns.
- **Practical Activity:** Use the `/educational` practice mode to analyze sample specimens (e.g., Banaba vs. Katmon leaves).
- **Assessment Criteria:** Student correctly identifies leaf arrangement (opposite vs. alternate) and compound leaflet structures with 90% accuracy.

---

## Module 2: Native vs. Invasive Philippine Flora
- **Learning Objective:** Distinguish Philippine endemic trees (e.g., Katmon) from native species (Narra, Banaba, Lagundi, Sambong) and introduced alien weeds (Tubang Bakod / Jatropha).
- **Practical Activity:** Conduct a campus/park tree survey and record native status using field observation logs.

---

## Module 3: Botanical Safety & Look-Alike Hazard Identification
- **Learning Objective:** Recognize poisonous plant species and distinguish them from look-alike herbal plants.
- **Key Safety Rule:** Identify *Jatropha curcas* (Tubang Bakod) seeds and leaves to prevent accidental toxic ingestion.

---

## Module 4: Digital Field Logging & Biodiversity Data Management
- **Learning Objective:** Collect geo-referenced plant observation data, measure tree height and DBH, and export standardized CSV reports.
